Colleen Hoover is back, and for some reason, I suffered through yet another one of her movies. A question I’m asking myself, now having seen three Colleen Hoover movies, is does she actually write characters that feel like real people? I swear the people in her films feel like they exist on a different planet to us.

In this movie, we are building up this idea of ambiguity as to whether or not this writer, Verity, has faked being paralysed or not. The thing is, the movie presents a pretty clear answer all the way through, and then just chucks a little bit on at the end to go, “Hmm, maybe."

Is this what constitutes a satisfying mystery? Because as far as I've read, this is an adaptation of her best book. If this is her best story, I really fear for those that willingly consume her writing.

The film posits that a mystery unfolds right before your eyes; however, despite those claims, by the end of the film, there is truly nothing shocking. Dakota Johnson, who is hired to be a ghostwriter for Verity, doesn't really have any reason to feel unsafe in this house despite the film trying to make you feel that way using little tricks from thrillers and horror movies. There are some truly laughable jump scares in this movie, with some of the most hilarious music beats.

It’s weird, things just happen because they are supposed to happen. Dakota Johnson starts to feel nervous in the house because she has to feel nervous for the story to continue. She’s not really given any reason to feel scared, bar the fact that she is reading this manuscript about Verity’s life, which paints her as a bad person. It doesn't help that Dakota Johnson gives the most flat performance here.

I truly don't understand what's going on in her head. She shows no emotion. She’s not a compelling character to take you on this journey. You just genuinely don't know what she feels, and when she suddenly feels scared, you're confused as there's been no progression.

They could have hammed up the campiness of this movie, and maybe it would have worked a little better, but they decided to play it real, and I find that a really weird choice. It’s also just not sexy. These movies try to present themselves as these erotic thrillers, but go back and watch erotic thrillers of previous decades, and they actually feel erotic and sexy. This movie does not.

This movie's a mess all the way through, with truly one of the most laughable endings I think I’ve ever seen in the history of cinema. I’m not exaggerating. I truly can't believe what I witnessed.
