LFF Film #15

This was one of my most anticipated of the year, and it's maybe one of my biggest disappointments. I say maybe because I don't know how I feel about this movie. This is not at all what I expected from this film, it being directed by Tony Gilroy and showcasing the life of a musician in LA. It is structurally so bizarre. I found it really hard to get my footing in how the story was being told, and when I did, I wasn't particularly sure it enhanced the story it was telling. Essentially, the movie is non-sequential. It’s all over the place. You have a present day where Pedro Pascal is playing film music, and that accompanies moments of his life. What’s genius about this structure is that it lets the film scores that Pedro Pascal is playing bleed into his real life, which enhances these maybe ordinary moments to feel like action films or thrillers. It genrefies his life and really hams up the intensity.

It is kind of the gimmick of the film that nine composers worked on it, and their signature scores can be felt throughout the film. But I think it amounts to very little, at least that's how I feel right now. We’re following such a passive protagonist in his life, and I didn't find him all that compelling. He is a wealthy nepo baby that comes from an industry family. Nothing in his world has weight. It’s a very weird world that's constructed, the way that characters speak and interact, and the way that he thinks about his own life. Maybe that is the point. Maybe it's shining a light on how these people operate. I just find it really bizarre. Coming off of Andor, which I think is one of the most significant television achievements of this century, I was expecting something a little more sharply written, but I found it so aimless, and pairing that with the jarring editing and the way that the story is told, it just feels so void of anything that it has to say. It looks gorgeous in moments, but it doesn't amount to anything. And by the time it ended, I really was deeply confused as to what I just watched. I will say though, anytime Olivia Wilde appeared on screen, there was an injection of energy that I sorely needed. I’m going to have to sit with this a while, but I didn't love this
